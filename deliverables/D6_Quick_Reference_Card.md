### IN-TRADE EXECUTION DISCIPLINE
* **Stop Placement:** Submit physical hard stop to the exchange immediately upon order fill.
* **Stop Modification:** NEVER widen a stop loss. NEVER move a stop to accommodate market noise.
* **Capital Protection:** NEVER average down. NEVER add to a losing position.
* **Chasing:** If price leaves your entry level, CANCEL the order. Do not chase.
* **Friction Awareness:** FYERS statutory friction (Brokerage, STT, GST, Exch) penalizes overtrading. Overtrading destroys edge.
