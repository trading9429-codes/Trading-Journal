# Daily Trading Checklist (D2)

**System:** Trader Execution Reset System  
**Version:** 1.0 (Client Calibrated)  
**Trading Window:** 09:15 to 10:30 AM IST  
**Minimum RR:** ≥ 2.2 RR  
**Broker Kill Switch:** Active  

---

## 1. Operating Instructions

This checklist is the operational gatekeeper of your trading day. Print multiple copies or maintain a fresh digital sheet daily.

* The 1-minute scanner generates candidate tickers; **it does not generate orders**.
* A trade is authorized **only** if all 5 items on the Per-Trade Checklist score a yes (5/5). A score of 4/5 is a strict **NO TRADE**.
* Keep this sheet next to your mouse and keyboard. You must physically tick each box prior to order transmission.

---

## 2. Detailed Checklist Breakdown

### Section 1: Pre-Market Preparation
1. **Daily Loss Limit Set:** Calculate your hard rupee amount based on 0.5%–1.0% account risk or 2R.
2. **Trading Window Confirmed:** Locked strictly to **09:15 to 10:30 AM IST**. Orders outside this 75-minute window are strictly forbidden.
3. **Emotional State Assessment:** Rate your calm/focus from 1 to 5. If scored below 4 (e.g., fatigue, anxiety, urgency, personal stress), do not trade today.
4. **Broker Kill Switch Active:** Confirm your broker's hard daily loss kill switch is activated in the platform.
5. **Macro / Corporate Events:** Verify scheduled earnings, monetary policy, or major economic data to avoid holding through binary announcements.
6. **Operating Intention:** Reiterate aloud: *“My job today is adherence, not recovery.”*

### Section 2: Per-Trade Evaluation (5/5 or No Trade)
1. **Scanner alert + setup matches written rules:** The ticker appeared on your verified scanner, candle closed on the 1-minute chart, and indicators meet defined entry criteria.
2. **Higher-timeframe context supports the trade:** 5-minute and 15-minute market structure aligns with trade direction (above key EMAs, trend direction, not pushing directly into major HTF resistance).
3. **Entry is at planned level — no chasing:** Limit order is stationed at your predetermined technical level. If price spikes past, you let it go.
4. **Stop is defined before entry and RR ≥ minimum:** Initial invalidation price is selected on technical structure before order entry. Expected reward divided by risk is **$\ge 2.2\text{ RR}$**.
5. **Within max trades/time window and no revenge/FOMO state:** You have taken fewer than your daily maximum trades, time is strictly within **09:15 to 10:30 AM**, and subjective emotional urgency is $\le 6/10$.

### Section 3: In-Trade Execution & Post-Trade Discipline
* **Order Type:** Limit orders only. Market orders are prohibited.
* **Stop Placement:** Physical stop-loss order submitted directly into the exchange order book immediately upon fill.
* **Capital Protection:** Never average down, never widen a stop loss, and never add to a losing position under any circumstance.
* **Post-Loss Protocol:** If stopped out, stand up from the workstation and begin an enforced 30-minute cooldown.
* **Termination Conditions:** Stop immediately for the day if 2 losses occur, if the broker kill switch triggers, or if any single written rule is violated.

### Section 4: End-of-Day Adherence Score (0 to 5)
Award 1 point for each completed item:
1. Stayed strictly within maximum trade allocation (2 trades in Phase 1–3, 3 trades in Phase 4–5).
2. Stopped trading immediately after 2 losses or hitting daily limit / broker kill switch.
3. Zero chasing, zero FOMO entries, and zero market order executions.
4. 100% of executed trades scored 5/5 on the pre-entry checklist with $\ge 2.2\text{ RR}$.
5. Fully journaled every trade with screenshot, FYERS statutory transaction charges, and emotional review.

* **Score 5/5 (100%):** Pass.
* **Score 4/5 (80%):** Warning. Review the failed point.
* **Score $\le$ 3/5 ($\le$ 60%):** Failure. Mandatory paper trading session the following day.
