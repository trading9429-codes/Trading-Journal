📊 Core Indicator Rules: The EMA Stack (10, 20, 50 EMA)

5-Minute Chart

Trend Filter: On the 5-minute chart, the 10, 20, and 50 EMAs define institutional trend control. Never take a long trade if the EMAs are tangled or in a bearish stack.

🟢 Bullish Trend Stack 10 > 20 > 50 EMA

All three EMAs sloping upwards. Price must be interacting cleanly with the 10 or 20 EMA.

🔴 Bearish Trend Stack 10 < 20 < 50 EMA

All three EMAs sloping downwards. Avoid longs completely; look for short continuum or stand aside.

⛔

The Mandatory Trend Prohibition

If price is below the 50 EMA, long setups are strictly prohibited regardless of volume spikes. Institutional order flow is not in your favor when below the 50 EMA baseline.

Setup A

VWAP & EMA Breakout (VWAP BO)

High-momentum breakout through VWAP resistance with expanding institutional volume

Condition: Stock consolidates above the 20 & 50 EMA near VWAP, then prints a 5-minute candle breaking resistance with volume ≥ 3x previous bar and 10 EMA > 20 EMA.
Entry Rule

Enter on the close of the breakout candle only when 10, 20, and 50 EMA are in a bullish stack.

Candle Trigger

Clean close across VWAP with expanding volume and closing range ≥ 0.7.

Stop Loss & Target

Hard stop placed strictly below the 20 EMA or breakout candle low. Target minimum 1:2 R:R (2.0R).

Setup B

5-Minute Volume Surge & EMA Continuation

Trend-continuation pullback after high-volume scanner surge with strict anti-chase discipline

Condition: Scanner triggers on volume ≥ 5x 20-period SMA, ADR ≥ 4%, with price trading well above the rising 50 EMA.
⚠️ Anti-Chase Mandate: NEVER chase initial spike candles that surge too far away from the 10 EMA. Wait for a mean-reversion pull toward the moving averages.
Candle Trigger

Bullish rejection wick off the 10 EMA or 20 EMA line during high volume.

Stop Loss & Risk Limit

Hard stop below the 50 EMA or swing low. Risk capped strictly at ₹2,500 per trade.

Setup C

The EMA & VWAP Pullback Reversal

High-probability mean-reversion entry on volume exhaustion at confluence support

Condition: Price surges, then pulls back precisely into the rising 10 EMA, 20 EMA, or VWAP zone on declining volume.
Entry Rule

Enter when pullback volume dries up and price taps the rising 10 or 20 EMA support zone.

Candle Trigger

Rejection wick or inside bar breakout confirming support at the 20 EMA.

Stop Loss & Quota

Hard stop placed strictly below the 50 EMA or pullback low. Max 2 trades per day total.
